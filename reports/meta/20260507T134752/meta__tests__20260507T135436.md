### 3. 4th category of bug only Meta would catch: FX meta['val'] staleness


**The bug**: `meta['val']` is populated by `torch.fx.Interpreter` during fake tracing. **Why only Meta catches this**: Grok/ChatGPT review Torch internals, but don’t track inductor’s `ShapeEnv` lifecycle. This is a TorchDynamo-specific invariant: `meta['val']` is not guaranteed post-spec.

**Concrete attacker scenario**: 
```python

```
